[ for institutions ]

Measure the team. Not the attendance.

Run red, blue and live red-vs-blue exercises for a whole cohort — every team on its own verified instance — and get a facilitated hotwash with an owned fix plan the same day. Offline if you need it. Defensible if you grade it.

// outcomes

Evidence, not vibes

Per-team and per-operator scorecards, time-to-detect and containment, a cohort leaderboard, and an after-action report you can export to Markdown or PDF, and trends across matches that show whether time-to-detect and containment are actually improving. LTI 1.3 posts grades back to your LMS.

// operations

One organizer, forty teams

Briefing cards to pick the scenario, one action to move every team from prep to live, white-cell injects, a cohort board for the room, and a one-click hotwash handoff when the clock stops.

// deployment

Runs where you must

Air-gapped, signed content bundles, no cloud dependency. Browser consoles for classrooms; an own-kit digital twin for SOCs that fight with their real tools.

// built for assessment

Grades you can defend.

Uncopyable per-team instancesEach team’s seed produces its own hosts, credentials and flags. Collusion yields wrong answers, and the integrity log names the source of a shared flag.
Verified before it’s servedAn independent solver recovers every flag from every instance first. You never grade a broken challenge.
Server-side gradingThe answer key never reaches a student’s browser. Live matches are scored on outcomes the mediation plane observed.
A journal for disputesEvery match persists with a tick-by-tick journal and a replay. “I did detect that” is settled by scrubbing to the frame.

// who runs it

One platform, four kinds of program.

// universities

Courses and labs

Rostered classes over LTI, a different seeded variant per student, scouting reports per learner and a cohort view per course. Runs in the lab with the cable unplugged.

// soc & ir teams

Pressure-test the floor

Monthly live red-vs-blue on a digital twin of your estate. Time-to-detect, containment and a fix plan with an owner you can action on Monday.

// mssps & trainers

Exercises as a deliverable

Repeatable seeded scenarios per client vertical, facilitated hotwashes, and an AAR plus replay that becomes the thing you hand over.

Event organizers get the same engine with a broadcast board: uncopyable flags, self-verifying challenges and no 2 a.m. “the flag’s broken” messages.

// content

Terrain your people will recognize.

Dozens of industry-accurate scenarios ship in the box — on-prem Active Directory, Kubernetes, SWIFT back office, Purdue-segmented OT, cloud identity, ransomware, insider, supply chain, MSP pivots — each fictional by construction and correct in its components, ports and kill-chain order. Author your own in the DSL and preview the briefing card and topology before anything is served.

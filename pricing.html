// licensing & deployment

The honest answers.

How are the prices set?

By the unit you budget for: a cohort-term for a course, a team-year for a SOC or a red team, a program-year for an own-kit twin, or a one-off event. The figures here are indicative launch pricing; the exact number follows cohort size, live-target needs and term length. There is no per-challenge or per-click metering, and no cloud cost hidden in a seat price — Hotwash runs on your hardware.

Does every tier run offline / air-gapped?

Yes. Targets, consoles and scoring run air-gapped on all tiers; challenge content ships as signed offline bundles, and nothing is pulled from the internet at match time. The Institution tier is designed to stand up inside an isolated range network.

Do all tiers grade the same way?

Yes. Grading is server-side on the mediation plane for every tier, so a result on the Team tier means the same thing as the same result on the Institution tier. Moving up a tier changes reach and realism, never the scoring.

Is there an education discount?

Education is its own tier priced per cohort-term, with a flat department license for unlimited courses, and public education and non-profits get 30% off Team. Individuals always have the free Community edition.

Can we author and run our own scenarios?

Yes — a documented DSL and authoring guide ship on every tier, and the engine validates and verifies every scenario (the independent solver must recover every flag) before it can be served.

[ for players ]

Play a range that can’t be gamed.

Every match you play is yours alone — seeded, verified, uncopyable. Real tools against real targets, scored on what you actually pull off, with your name on the scorecard. Then a hotwash that tells you exactly what to fix.

// red

Hands on keyboard

Recon to objective across a seeded network. No two runs are the same, so write-ups don’t help and nobody can hand you the flag.

// blue

Catch it for real

Write a Sigma, YARA or Suricata-style rule and get graded on planted malicious plus benign data. Zero false positives, or it doesn’t count.

// red vs blue

Fight a live opponent

Both cells on one board. You’re scored on outcomes the mediation plane can see — footholds, captures, detections, evictions — not on which buttons you clicked.

// your record

Your record follows you.

Hotwash names operators. Every foothold, capture, detection and containment you make is credited to you — on the live board, in the replay and in the after-action report.

Operators panelThe observer board ranks named red and blue operators by contribution while the match is live.
Per-operator scorecardThe hotwash breaks the team’s score down to you: access, stealth, detection, response.
Replay scrubberDrag through any finished match frame by frame and study the moment it turned.
Key momentsFirst blood, the jewel falling, the cut-off, the lead change — the story of the match, with your name on the beats you made.

// fair by construction

Nobody gets to cheat. Including the platform.

Uncopyable flagsYour flags come from your seed. A flag copied from another team is simply wrong — and the log says who leaked it.
Verified before you see itAn independent solver recovers every flag from your instance before it’s served. No broken challenges, no 2 a.m. “is this one solvable?”
Graded server-sideThe answer key never reaches a browser. What you submit is checked against what was generated for you.
Outcomes, not keystrokesIn a live match the plane scores a seeded proof you can’t read without real compromise, real detections, real eviction.

// learn from every fight

The debrief is the point.

Every match ends in a hotwash written from the journal: the five questions, what decided it, and a prioritized fix plan. Download it as Markdown, print it, watch the replay. In the consoles, press ? — the shortcuts keep you fast when it counts.

// bring your own kit

Fight with your real stack

On the own-kit tier you work from your own offensive or defensive tooling against container- or VM-backed infrastructure, with telemetry flowing into your SIEM.

// three scenarios on the site

Watch before you play

The demo board runs three different matches — a razor-thin blue win, a red blitz, a clean hold. Pick one and watch how a match reads.

Industry-accurate terrain

Active Directory, Kubernetes, payments, OT, cloud, ransomware, insider — built with the right components, ports and kill-chain order.
